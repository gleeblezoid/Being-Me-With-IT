# Auto-captioning

## Zoom

Zoom contains a setting which you can enable to provide auto-captioning for your meetings and other Zoom events. The steps to enable captioning are outlined in Zoom's knowledge-base [here](https://support.zoom.us/hc/en-us/articles/207279736-Getting-Started-with-Closed-Captioning)

## Maestra

There is a free browser based AI driven tool called [Maestra](https://live.maestra.ai/) available - read the Maestra [privacy policy](https://maestra.ai/privacy-policy) if intended for business or sensitive usecases where data residency or LLM usage might be of concern.
